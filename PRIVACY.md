# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Himawari ("the Bot") handles your data. By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Data Collection
We only collect the absolute minimum data required for the Bot to function properly. This includes:
- **Discord User IDs & Guild (Server) IDs**: Used to track user preferences, cooldowns, and server-specific configurations.
- **Message Content**: When you interact with the AI chat feature, your messages are temporarily processed to generate a response. **We do not permanently store your chat logs.** Only the last few messages in an active channel are temporarily kept in memory to maintain conversation context, and they are deleted when the server restarts or memory fills up.
- **Voice Channel Data**: When using voice commands, the bot temporarily processes your voice channel state to join or move users.

## 2. Data Usage
The collected data is used exclusively for:
- Providing the requested features (e.g., AI chat, music playback, moderation).
- Improving the Bot's responses and maintaining context during a conversation.
- Preventing abuse (e.g., rate limiting and cooldowns).

## 3. Data Sharing
**We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to anyone.**
- Chat messages processed by the AI feature are securely transmitted to our AI providers (Groq and Google Gemini) via encrypted API requests solely for the purpose of generating a reply. These providers do not use this data to train their public models.

## 4. Data Security
We implement standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access or alteration of the minimal data we store. The database (MongoDB) is secured and requires authentication.

## 5. User Rights (Data Deletion)
If you wish to have your data removed from our database, please contact the bot owner (Ayushh) directly on Discord.

## 6. Changes to this Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Continued use of the Bot after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the new policy.
